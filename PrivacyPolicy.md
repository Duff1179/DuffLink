Privacy Policy



DuffLink Privacy Policy



Effective Date: August 3, 2026



1\. Overview



DuffLink is an application that connects to ProPresenter® on a local network to display stage information on Roku devices, phones, tablets, and web browsers.



DuffLink is designed to operate entirely within your local network.



2\. Information We Collect



DuffLink does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personally identifiable information.



The application does not require:



User accounts

User registration

Email addresses

Passwords

Payment information

Location information

3\. Local Network Communication



DuffLink communicates only with:



A ProPresenter instance that you specify on your local network.

Client devices (such as Roku devices, phones, tablets, or computers) connected to the same local network.



This communication remains within your local network and is not transmitted to DuffLink's developer.



4\. Information Displayed



DuffLink may display information received from ProPresenter, including:



Current slide text

Next slide text

Presentation title

Remaining slide count

Active timer information

Elapsed service time



This information is provided solely for display on connected devices and is not stored or transmitted outside your local network by DuffLink.



5\. Third-Party Services



DuffLink does not use analytics, advertising services, or cloud-based data collection.



Roku or your device manufacturer may collect diagnostic or usage information according to their own privacy policies.



6\. Data Security



Because DuffLink operates locally, users are responsible for securing their own network environment.



7\. Children's Privacy



DuffLink is not directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.



8\. Changes to This Policy



This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of DuffLink after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
